Total project cost. Trigger at the lesser of $300,000 or 10% — for this $18.4M project that is $300,000. Current variance +$41,200. Headroom $258,800.
§ 95.611(c)(2)(ii)(F)45 CFR
Cost-benefit estimate. Trigger at more than 10% change. Current variance −2.4%. This trigger exists only on the enhanced track; the regular track has no equivalent.
§ 95.611(b)(2)(iv)45 CFR
Contract amendments. Prior approval required at a cost increase over $500,000 or a term extension over 60 days. Two active contracts. No amendment pending.
The 60-day prong is the one states miss. A no-cost 90-day extension still requires prior approval.

Cost methodology. No change since approval. Note the enhanced text says “cost methodology”; it is the regular track that says “cost allocation methodology.”
